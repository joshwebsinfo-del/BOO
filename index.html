Healing & Relaxation

Traditional Hot Stone Baths & Wellness

Bhutanese Menchu (Hot Stone Bath)

The traditional Bhutanese Hot Stone Bath is an ancient healing ritual practiced for centuries. It uses pristine mountain river stones that are heated on log fires until they glow red hot. The stones are then placed into a wooden tub filled with mountain water, releasing rich therapeutic minerals.

We infuse our hot stone baths with fresh Mugwort leaves (Khempa), which releases a sweet medicinal aroma. This helps relieve joint pains, muscle stiffness, skin diseases, and deep-seated mental fatigue.
